How I actually work

How I actually workJEDUF

Just enough design up front.

Between rigid waterfall and pure chaos. The middle path is where the speed compounds without the mess.

01 · Too much
Waterfall
“Plan enough and you won’t get hurt.”
Requirements→Design→Build→Release
Slow or stuck. High quality when it ships, if it ever ships.
02 · Just right
Spec-driven
“Think upfront about where you’re heading, then find the path.”
Spec→Build→Iterate→Ship
Fast with focus. Quality and direction stay intact.
03 · Too little
Vibe coding
“Dive in, YOLO mode. You’ll figure it out.”
Prompt→Accept all→Prompt again→Hope
Fast but fragile. No direction, no quality control, pure luck.

The trick

One spec drives everything.

Write the idea down clearly. The agent reads it, knows the context, executes against it. The spec is what turns a few hours into something real.
